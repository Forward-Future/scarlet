# Visual pass

Add cutaways, supplied images, graphics or labels over an edit whose dialogue timing stays as it is. The brief and the surrounding footage decide the assets, placement, duration and sound.

A visual should show what is being said, or what the viewer would want to see while it is said. Bring it in on the words that call for it and hold it long enough to take in, usually a few seconds, longer for something to read. Return to the speaker for the moments their face carries the meaning: a joke, an admission, a strong opinion.

Use the editor's own images, screenshots and footage where they exist, as they are. Don't add titles, labels or graphics the brief didn't ask for. Keep each addition on a track of its own, so the editor can move or remove it without touching the edit under it.

Moving screen footage stays moving footage; framing it is the screen-framing skill's work.

`frame_contact_sheet`, `screen_text`, `asset_add` and `timeline_propose` cover what this needs.
