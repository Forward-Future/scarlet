# Caption the edit

Caption the speech that is still in the edit, in short, readable lines. The brief and the picture decide wording, line breaks, placement and style.

`captions_generate` builds a caption track from the kept words. `timeline_propose` edits individual cues: a selected passage, a correction or hand-written lines. `frame_contact_sheet` shows the captions composed on a version.
