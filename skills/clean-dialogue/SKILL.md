# Clean dialogue

Correct sustained noise, tone or level on a microphone. The recording and the delivery brief decide the processing and its settings.

`media_profile` measures a source's room floor, speech level and loudness, so a processed passage can be compared with the original by measurement. `waveform_get` gives levels over a passage, and `source_audio` plays one in sessions that receive audio. `timeline_propose` sets a clip's gain and effects.
